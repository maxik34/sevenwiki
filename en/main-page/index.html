Welcome to the Seven Wiki

This is an independent fan-project dedicated to the detailed study of Windows 7. It contains technical details about the system's design, modifications, and history.

Some Notes

Right now, the site is in its early stages, so it is currently more of a concept than a wiki. I think once I implement search, a new site builder, and a decent number of articles, it can be considered a wiki.

Articles roadmap

  • engine/ – An article about various Windows technologies and engines.
  • engine/directui/ – An article about DirectUI and the DUIXML format used in certain Windows interfaces.
  • file-formats/ – An article about various file formats in general.
  • file-formats/.msstyles/ – A detailed article on the .msstyles file format.
  • main-page/ – Website homepage (you are currently on it).
  • site/ – A technical page for wiki maintenance (similar to Special:SpecialPages in the MediaWiki engine).
  • site/search/ – Site search page.
  • updates/ – An article about Windows updates in general.
  • updates/kb2505438/ – An article detailing a specific update.
  • windows-7/ – An article about Windows 7 in general.
  • windows-7/files/ – An article about Windows 7 files in general.
  • windows-7/files/aero.msstyles/ – A detailed article about the aero.msstyles file.
  • windows-7/files/aero.msstyles/structure/ – Article detailing the complete file structure (classes, parts, states, and properties).
  • windows-7/files/write.exe/ – A detailed article about the write.exe file.
  • windows-7/folders/ – An article about Windows 7 folders in general.
